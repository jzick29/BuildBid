# The True Cost of Estimating Errors for Trade Contractors

**August 1, 2026** | Estimating Best Practices

A single missed line item can wipe out the profit on an entire job. The average contractor loses 5-10% of revenue to estimating errors annually.

## The Most Common Errors

Missing line items, inconsistent markups, outdated material pricing, and labor hour underestimation account for most estimating losses.

## The Compounding Effect

Errors don't just cost you on one job — they compound into lost bids, underpriced work, and lower customer expectations.

## How to Fix It

Pre-built templates, consistent markups, and actual-vs-estimated cost tracking eliminate the most common errors.
